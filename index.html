CORE SYSTEM

Familiar rules. A new frontier.

The Far Reach uses a familiar d20 foundation: six abilities, proficiency, saving throws, Defence, hit points, actions, reactions and advantage/disadvantage. All setting content, careers, peoples, technology and adventures are original.

PLAYABLE PEOPLES

Not every mind evolved alone.

Humans

Descendants of the lost Crossing. Adaptable, scattered, and divided over whether Earth was history or myth.

Khelt

Colonies of countless organisms inhabiting a growing mineral skeleton. To a Khelt, “I” means the colony.

Orrin

Sightless electromagnetic sensates who perceive nervous systems and powered technology as luminous structures.

Velari

Partially fluid beings whose flexible lattice bodies change dramatically under different gravity.

Naruun

Dual-brained beings whose conscious and instinctive minds process the universe in parallel.

Echoed

Biological memory-organisms housed in artificial shells. Rebirth preserves memories—but never perfectly.
