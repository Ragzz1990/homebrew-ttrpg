SETTING LORE

The Crossing

FOUNDATIONAL LORE

Humanity did not conquer this galaxy. Humanity arrived late.

Thousands of years ago, colony vessels carrying humans appeared in a galaxy unimaginably distant from the Milky Way. The event became known as The Crossing.

No surviving record explains who transported the ships, what technology was used or why humanity was chosen. No civilisation has successfully recreated the journey.

Earth became history, then legend, then argument. Some humans consider it humanity's birthplace. Some believe it was a paradise. Others teach that humanity was exiled from it.

What are the Vein Gates?

Vein Gates are ancient transit structures connecting distant regions of the galaxy. Modern civilisations know how to operate many of them, but not how they were originally constructed. The farther a vessel travels from established space, the more incomplete, unstable or unknown the network becomes.

What is the Far Reach?

The Far Reach is the frontier beyond dependable routes: unclaimed systems, pirate domains, corporate colonies, independent stations, lost ships, alien ruins and civilisations that have never entered the wider galactic order.

BODY MODIFICATION

Cybernetics

LEGALITY VARIES BY SYSTEM

Cybernetics are progression, equipment and identity. Characters can replace, reinforce or expand parts of their bodies, but powerful augmentation has costs beyond credits.

NEURALOCULARARMSTORSOLEGSDERMAL

Augmentation has consequences

Illegal implants can trigger security alerts. Military hardware may require licences. EMP effects can threaten exposed systems. Corporate implants can carry proprietary locks. Species compatibility matters. Black-market modifications may contain stolen identifiers—or unknown software.
