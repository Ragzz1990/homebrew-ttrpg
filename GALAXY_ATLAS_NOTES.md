# Galaxy Atlas v1.2

The playable setting is now fictionally located inside **NGC 6872**, the Condor Galaxy, roughly 212 million light-years from Earth. NASA describes NGC 6872 as a giant barred spiral roughly 522,000 light-years across. The real galaxy is only the astronomical anchor; every named planet, civilisation, route and settlement in The Far Reach is fictional.

Atlas entries: 43

The SVG artwork is original procedural vector art generated specifically for this build, so the website has no dependence on third-party sci-fi illustrations.
